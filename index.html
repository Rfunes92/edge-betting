How to read this

American odds

-150 means you bet $150 to win $100 (the favorite). +130 means a $100 bet wins $130 (the underdog). The number also gives the chance the price implies: -150 is 60%, +130 is 43%.

Spread: -3.5 means that team has to win by 4 or more. +3.5 means that team covers if it loses by 3 or fewer, or wins. Total 44.5: Over wins if both teams combine for 45+ points.

Units

A unit is your standard bet: 1–2% of your bankroll (set in Settings). Most bets are 1 unit. Even a strong edge stays at 2 units max. Same size after a win or a loss, so no single bet can hurt you.

Break-even

At -110 you have to win 52.4% of your bets just to break even, because the book keeps a cut. Going 50/50 at -110 loses money over time.

CLV (closing line value)

Compare the price you got with the price right before kickoff (the "closing line"). If you took Eagles -2.5 and it closed -3.5, you beat the close. Doing that again and again is the best sign you have a real edge. One week of wins or losses tells you very little.

Line shopping

DraftKings and FanDuel often post different prices for the same bet. Getting -105 instead of -115 lowers your break-even from 53.5% to 51.2%. Always check both books before betting. The Line shop tab does the math.

Why we pass

Most games don't offer an edge. Passing costs nothing. Forcing a bet on a weak slate pays the house's cut for no reason. A week with zero picks is a good outcome when nothing stands out.
